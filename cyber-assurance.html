Cyber Security / vCISO

Protecting their data. Proving you can recover.

Department: Infection Control and Immunology

Cyber Essentials is now a formal gateway requirement on most public-sector contracts. Miss it, and you are excluded before price is even considered.

What it costs you to leave alone

Without Cyber Essentials, a buyer assumes unmanaged devices and networks, unpatched systems, no incident response capability, weak data handling and you are a soft entry point into their supply chain.

That last one is the killer. An OEM will not share sensitive design data with a supplier it thinks is leaky.

The point most people miss

Most manufacturers think of cyber security as an IT problem. It is a governance problem, and Cyber Essentials is how a buyer checks your governance without ever needing to meet you.

A buyer will not hand sensitive data or system access to a business that cannot prove it is defended.

What it covers

Cyber Assurance

Cyber Essentials, Cyber Essentials Plus, ISO 27001 alignment, penetration testing and vulnerability management.

Network & Access Security

Patch management, multi-factor authentication, access control, segmentation, firewalls and email protection.

Data Protection & Privacy

UK GDPR, DPIAs, data retention, secure deletion and lawful handling of personal information.

Response & Recovery

Incident planning, tested backups, staff awareness, supplier assurance and evidence of recovery capability.

What it looks like inside a manufacturer

  • Firewalls, endpoints and cloud services are configured to a certified standard.
  • Multi-factor authentication is enforced on every account, including the directors'.
  • Software and firmware are patched on a schedule, not reactively.
  • Admin rights are restricted to the people who actually need them.
  • Staff can recognise and report phishing attempts.
  • A named person owns cyber risk at board level.
  • Backups are encrypted and, more importantly, tested.
  • An incident response plan exists and has been rehearsed.
  • Certification renews on schedule, not lapses unnoticed.
