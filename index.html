The Timing Problem

The problem isn’t certification.
It’s when you start.

Reactive compliance — triggered by a tender you’ve already found — almost always arrives too late. Every certification has a lead time. Tender deadlines don’t wait.

Cyber Essentials (Basic)4–8 weeks
GDPR Alignment Programme4–8 weeks
Cyber Essentials Plus2–3 months
ISO 9001 Certification3–6 months
ISO 45001 / ISO 140014–6 months
ISO 27001 Certification6–12 months
A manufacturer can see a contract coming six months out — exactly the lead time needed. The ones winning the work treat compliance as a planned business function, not a last-minute checklist triggered by a rejection letter.

Where it started

Two sides of the same table

I grew up in Washington DC. My stepfather adopted me when I was seven and I became Robert Atkins Jr., and I worshipped him. My mother ran procurement for the DC public schools; he bid for her contract, he won it, and eventually he won her too. He built a typewriter repair business from nothing and gave a hundred young men from tough streets a real trade. In five years we went from sharing one tin of tomato soup to a six-bedroom house with a pool, and by 1974 the business had $16.9 million in the bank.

I've spent thirty years in enterprise sales at IBM, HP and BT, and I once turned a single laptop into a £2.6 million a year account with the Ford Tractor Plant (now Case New Holland) in Basildon. What I took from all of it is what my parents already knew:

Being good at the work gets you into the room. Being ready is what wins the contract.

Why I started Your Best Solution

One password. 700 jobs.

In June 2025 I sat in a room at the Gherkin at a CyberSmart event and heard how KNP Logistics (opens the BBC report in a new tab), a 158-year-old firm turning over roughly £100 million, lost 700 jobs after attackers got in through one employee's password. It wasn't a clever attack, just one credential. I'd already spent thirty years watching capable manufacturers lose ground, not on what they could deliver but on what they couldn't prove: Cyber Essentials, ISO, health and safety, GDPR. Buyers work through a checklist before they ever look at your price. Most manufacturers with 10 to 500 people are ticking those boxes alone, one specialist at a time, and often after the tender has closed. So I started Your Best Solution.

Manufacturers don't lose contracts because they can't do the work. They lose them because they can't prove they're ready.

The Cure Alliance

One coordinator. The right specialist at the right time.

That's where the Cure Alliance comes in. I'm not one consultant pretending to be expert in everything. I'm the hospital administrator. I run a Compliance Health Check, look at your tender through the buyer's eyes, and bring in the right vetted specialist at the right time, whether that's cyber, ISO, health and safety, GDPR or insurance. You get one point of contact, one clear roadmap and one conversation instead of six, and nothing falls through the cracks. Does that make sense?
