HR & Employment Law

HR, employment law and social value for tenders Proving your people are managed, protected and legally covered.

Department: Clinical Education and Staff Competency

Social value is now weighted heavily on manufacturing tenders, and HR evidence is where most of those marks live or die.

What leaving HR and social value alone costs you

Without current HR and employment documentation, a buyer assumes weak or absent EDI commitment, unaudited, higher-risk supply chain on modern slavery, IR35 and worker classification exposure, generic, unsubstantiated wellbeing claims and social value answers that won't survive scrutiny.

None of that may be true of you. It does not matter. In a tender, the assumption is what gets scored.

The HR and social value point most manufacturers miss

Most manufacturers have decent practice and no documentation. To an evaluator, undocumented good practice scores identically to having no practice at all.

This is not HR administration. It is operational risk control, and buyers score it that way.

What HR and social value support covers

EDI & Modern Slavery

Equality, diversity and inclusion policy plus a Modern Slavery statement backed by a real supply-chain audit.

Contracts & Classification

Employment contract review and IR35 worker classification that holds up to scrutiny.

Pay & Wellbeing

Real Living Wage accreditation and wellbeing programmes with substance behind them, not slogans.

Governance

TUPE readiness, right-to-work checks, grievance procedures and whistleblowing policy.

What good HR and social value look like inside a manufacturer

  • EDI policy exists, is current, and has a genuine action plan behind it.
  • Modern Slavery statement is published and reflects an actual supply chain audit.
  • Employment contracts are compliant and worker classification is defensible.
  • Wellbeing initiatives are real and can be described with specifics, not slogans.
  • TUPE processes are understood and ready before a contract requires them.
  • Right-to-work checks are consistent and recorded.
  • Grievance processes are documented and followed when used.
  • HR documentation is reviewed annually, not written once and forgotten.

Common questions about HR and employment law

Do I need a Modern Slavery statement?

It is a legal requirement if your annual turnover is £36 million or more, under section 54 of the Modern Slavery Act 2015. Many buyers ask smaller suppliers for one anyway, or for a description of how they check their supply chain. Publish it on your website, have a director approve it, and update it every year.

How do HR policies score in social value?

They are where much of the social value score is evidenced. Central government gives social value at least a 10% weighting under PPN 002, and evaluators look for specifics: Real Living Wage, apprenticeships, an equality action plan with targets, and wellbeing support you can describe in detail. Good practice that is not written down scores the same as no practice at all.

What HR documents should a manufacturer have before bidding?

An equality, diversity and inclusion policy, a Modern Slavery statement, whistleblowing, grievance and disciplinary procedures, compliant employment contracts and consistent right-to-work records. If the contract could involve staff transferring, add a TUPE plan. Review the set once a year.
